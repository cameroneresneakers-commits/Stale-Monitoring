Data lives in this browser's local storage — nothing is sent anywhere. Use "Export backup" regularly, since local storage can be cleared by the browser or lost if you switch machines; "Import backup" restores it.
A unit counts as stale here if its base SKU's most recent receiving date (from the receiving history export) is 31+ days before today. SKUs sold but not found anywhere in the receiving history export are auto-bucketed Liquidate 120+ (marked with an asterisk in the SKU table) since no recent receiving record is itself a stale signal. 0–30 days since received is current stock and is excluded from all totals on this page. No revenue/COGS or platform breakdown is available from these source files, so this monitor is units-only, by brand.
